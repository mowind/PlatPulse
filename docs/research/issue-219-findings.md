# Issue #219 findings — Validator snapshot trends and the real day/month boundaries

**#219** ("feat: Validator 快照趋势与真实日/月边界", parent #202, stories 64, 67 and 68) asks for three things that the design keeps apart on purpose. The first is a **trend read**: rank, stake and delegator count per day, over a bounded range, for one Validator (story 64). The second is a **calendar boundary**: those days and months are the *configured* IANA calendar's days and months, and every boundary has to be mapped back into the UTC coordinate the rest of the product investigates in, without the Server ever re-bucketing a stored row into another zone (story 67). The third is **continuity across a Node deletion**: a Node's Purge must not take the shared Validator's retained history with it, and the association rows that Purge does delete have to read back as *unavailable here* rather than as *never existed* (story 68). The two risks the ticket names are the reason the shape is what it is: "累计数冒称周期收益" (a cumulative counter read as period earnings) and "UTC 重桶错误改变 calendar 归属" (a silent UTC re-bucket moving a row to a different day).

Everything below is the working tree as it stands, read at the line references given. It is not a plan, and it makes no claim about a run this pass did not perform (see VERIFICATION).

## SERVER — the gap, then the read

1. **The buckets were already written in the configured zone; nothing read them back with their real boundaries.** Written rows carry a `timezone`, `local_date` and `month_key` chosen by `pub fn analytics_period(observation: &ValidatorObservation, received_at: &str, timezone: &str) -> Result<(String, String, String), ValidatorError>` (crates/platpulse-server/src/validator.rs:1989) — Provider timestamp preferred, Server receipt time as the fallback — and by `pub fn local_period_at(...)` (:2273). The Admin reads that existed took only a row `limit` (`pub async fn list_daily_snapshots` :2012, `pub async fn list_monthly_aggregates` :2026), so an Owner could see rows but not a window, a cursor, a coverage verdict, or the UTC instants those local days actually cover.
2. **The read this ticket adds** is `pub async fn load_daily_trend(db: &ServerDatabase, query: ValidatorTrendQuery, now: OffsetDateTime) -> Result<ValidatorTrendPage, ValidatorError>` (crates/platpulse-server/src/validator.rs:2337), with `ValidatorTrendQuery` (:2056), `ValidatorTrendPoint` (:2072), `ValidatorTrendGap` (:2108), `ValidatorTrendMonth` (:2119) and `ValidatorTrendPage` (:2145). `from`/`to` are Server-owned instants, never bucket boundaries: each one is resolved to the configured local date **its instant falls in**, and `to` therefore includes its own local day. `from` defaults to `to` minus 90 days (`TREND_DEFAULT_WINDOW_DAYS` :2044), `to` defaults to `now`, and `from > to` is `ValidatorError::InvalidTrendWindow` ("from must not be later than to") — a 400, not an empty trend.
3. **The calendar is the configuration's, and a DST day is 23 or 25 hours wide.** `fn local_midnight(zone: &Tz, date: chrono::NaiveDate) -> Result<OffsetDateTime, ValidatorError>` (:2205) walks the local hours 0:00 → 1:00 → 2:00 and takes the earliest `Ambiguous` reading, so a local midnight a daylight-saving transition erased still lands on the true local day instead of silently sliding a day forward; `pub fn local_day_bounds` (:2243) and `pub fn local_date_at` (:2260) are the shared helpers. Every point carries the UTC instants that day really covers (`dayStart` inclusive, `dayEnd` exclusive), each month carries its own `monthStart`/`monthEnd` (:1041-1043), and the zone itself travels with the answer (`timezone`), so a reader can reconstruct the boundary without re-deriving it.
4. **The query is bounded, measured, and says what it narrowed.** The window is clamped to `TREND_MAX_WINDOW_DAYS` = 730 (:2045) and reports `clamped`; `limit` defaults to `TREND_DEFAULT_LIMIT` = 90 (:2046) and is clamped to `TREND_MAX_LIMIT` = 366 (:2047); the SQL asks for `limit + 1` rows to decide `truncated` honestly, then reverses to ascending local-date order, so truncation drops the **oldest** days and never silently drops the newest. Paging is a **cursor**, not an offset: `before` is a configured local date, resolved through `pred_opt()` so only strictly older days answer, and the response hands the next cursor back in `continuation` (`null` when the answer is not truncated). `expectedDays`/`observedDays`/`missingDays` are computed over the *answered* stretch only, so days outside the page are not misreported as silences; `gaps` are edge-triggered and carry their day count; `firstObservedLocalDate`/`lastObservedLocalDate` are `null` rather than a fabricated day.
5. **Coverage distinguishes "nothing was asked" from "nothing was observed".** `fn trend_coverage(page: &validator::ValidatorTrendPage) -> &'static str` (crates/platpulse-server/src/http/validators_admin.rs:1127) answers `empty` when the stretch holds no days, `unavailable` when days were expected and none were observed, `complete` only when nothing is missing, truncated or clamped, and `partial` otherwise. `unavailable` is deliberately not `0`: a Provider that answered nothing is not evidence of zero stake.
6. **Sample provenance travels with each row.** `sampleTime` is `provider` or `receipt` — which timestamp chose that day — beside both raw timestamps; `delaySeconds` comes from `pub fn observed_delay_seconds` (:2289) and is `Option`, `null` when the row carried no Provider timestamp, **never `0` for an unknown delay**; `clockSuspect` marks a Provider timestamp that is ahead of its receipt. Missing metrics are `null` (Unknown) at every level, and no field in the DTO turns an absence into a zero.
7. **The counters are cumulative, and the contract says so.** `counterSemantics` is the fixed string `"cumulative"` (:1081 and the response builder), documented as "never derives period earnings, net profit, or a silently UTC re-bucketed series"; the trend only ever republishes the stored `reward_amount`/`block_count` values. There is no differencing or rate arithmetic anywhere in `load_daily_trend`.
8. **A row stored in another zone is disclosed, never merged and never re-bucketed.** The page query filters `validator_id = ? AND timezone = ?`, and rows of the same Validator under other zones are counted in their own query as `foreignRows`/`foreignTimezones` with the zones named. A same-day foreign row is therefore *not* merged into the configured day, and the configured day keeps reading as a silence — the honest answer for a coordinate that really has no row.
9. **Story 68 is answered on the read side too.** `pub async fn list_validator_associations` (:2302) joins `nodes`, so an association whose Node was purged simply cannot resolve and is never re-attached to a surviving Node; `pub async fn count_deleted_nodes` (:2318) reports how many of the Validator's Network Nodes Purge already deleted, capped association listing is disclosed through `associationsTruncated` (`TREND_MAX_ASSOCIATIONS` = 100 :2049), and `associationHistoryPartial` says the list is not the whole history. The **retained snapshot days keep answering** after a purge: `validator_daily_snapshots` is keyed by Validator, not by Node.
10. **The rank column was a real gap this ticket had to close.** `record_daily_snapshot` (:2583, whose `INSERT` column list has no `rank`) stores one row per (Validator, zone, local day) and only ever raises it — the `ON CONFLICT ... DO UPDATE ... WHERE excluded.sample_at > ... OR (equal AND excluded.observation_key > ...)` tie rule means a newer sample wins and an identical one is a no-op. Rank arrives in the *same* refresh cycle from a different Provider call, so before this ticket it could not reach an already-stored day at all. `record_snapshot_rank` (:2620) now writes it inside the same refresh transaction, and returns early when `rows_affected() == 0`: that guard is load-bearing, because `rebuild_monthly_aggregate` (:2644) has no zero-row protection and would otherwise insert a bogus `snapshot_count = 0` aggregate for a month nobody observed. `apply_ranking_result` (:3408) writes the ranked value, writes **`NULL` for an authoritative absence** (the cohort answered and this Validator is not in it), and leaves the row untouched on `NotConfigured`/`Unsupported`/`Error` — a failure keeps last-good instead of fabricating either a rank or an absence.
11. **The Admin surface.** `ValidatorTrendRequest{from,to,before,limit}` (:976), the browser-facing DTOs `:992-1120` (camelCase, with the field docs quoted above), `fn trend_coverage` (:1127) and `pub(crate) async fn admin_validator_trend` (:1152) at `GET /api/admin/v1/validators/{validator_id}/trend`. It is merged into the `/api/admin/v1` Admin router, so it inherits the Owner-only guard from the nesting (crates/platpulse-server/src/http/mod.rs) with no bespoke auth, and database failures stay 503 through `error_response` (:304), which maps both `InvalidTimezone` and `InvalidTrendWindow` to 400 `invalid_request` (:326-327). The configured zone reaches the handler through `AppState::with_validator_timezone`, wired from `[validator_provider] timezone` in `cli.rs`; `config.rs` rejects a zone that does not parse as IANA (`ConfigError::InvalidValidatorTimezone`), so the read can never silently fall back to UTC.

## WEB — the Owner can now read the trend and its boundaries

- `platpulse-web/src/validatorTrend.ts` is the pure presentation module (no React, unit tested): `VALIDATOR_TREND_PAGE_DAYS` (:49), `VALIDATOR_TREND_PRESETS` (:63 — 7/30/90/180/365 days), `validatorTrendRange` (:73 — whole-second UTC bounds, so a reload of the same window is the same query), `VALIDATOR_TREND_SERIES` (:85 — rank, stake, delegator count, in that order), `validatorTrendValue` (:97), `validatorTrendCoverage` (:144), the disclosure sentences `validatorTrendStretchNotice` (:159), `validatorTrendTruncationNotice` (:186), `validatorTrendForeignNotice` (:204), `validatorTrendAssociationNotice` (:220), `validatorTrendCounterNotice` (:239) and `validatorTrendGapNotice` (:253), plus `validatorTrendSampleTimeLabel` (:270), `validatorTrendDelay` (:280 — absent or non-finite reads "Unknown", never "0s"), `validatorTrendDayWindow` (:304 — states that a DST day is 23 or 25 hours wide), `validatorTrendMonthWindow` (:315), `validatorTrendDayOrdinal` (:322), and the chart geometry `validatorTrendChartGeometry` (:378) / `validatorTrendLinePath` (:455) that draws silences as banded gaps.
- **The presets deliberately do not all ask for their whole range.** `VALIDATOR_TREND_PAGE_DAYS` = 31 caps a single page at 31 days while the presets still span up to 365, which is what makes "Load older days" a *reachable* Owner path and keeps a long window from turning into a longer response. Every preset stays within the Server's own 366 limit, and a preset's `limit` is never larger than its day count plus one (the inclusive `to` boundary).
- `platpulse-web/src/pages/validatorTrendPanel.tsx` is the panel itself: `useValidatorTrendWindow` (:84) owns series, preset, cursor and the current query, and `ValidatorTrendPanel` (:305) renders the intro, the series selector, the preset buttons, the disclosure paragraphs, a `dl` of answered counts, the chart, the paging controls, the gap list, the stored-days table, the configured-months table and the Node-association list. Selectors are `data-slot`-addressed for the acceptance spec (`validator-trend-panel`, `-coverage`, `-stretch`, `-truncated`, `-foreign`, `-associations-notice`, `-counters`, `-chart`, `-gaps`, `-no-gaps`, `-points`, `-empty`, `-months`, `-association-list`, `-no-associations`), interactive targets keep the 44px minimum, and the empty states are stated in words ("No configured local day of the answered stretch is missing a snapshot"; deleted associations are "unavailable rather than never having existed").
- `platpulse-web/src/pages/AdminValidators.tsx` mounts it at the end of the Validator detail (`:41` import, `:525` render), so the trend sits beside the panels #218 added and reads the same generation-keyed cache. `platpulse-web/src/api/admin.ts` adds `fetchAdminValidatorTrend`/`useAdminValidatorTrend` (:1059) keyed on the Validator plus the window and the realtime `generation`, so an identity change invalidates the trend the same way it invalidates the rest of the detail.
- `docs/openapi/openapi.json` was regenerated from the Server itself (one new path, five new schemas) and the strict client was regenerated from it (`adminValidatorTrend` in `platpulse-web/src/api/generated/sdk.gen.ts:919`), so the WebUI calls the typed client rather than hand-written fetch.

## What this ticket does not claim

- It does **not** add periodic earnings, net profit, or any derived yield. `counterSemantics` is the contract: the reward and block values are cumulative Provider counters as of each sample, and the UI labels them that way.
- It does **not** backfill or re-bucket history. Days stored under another zone stay where they are and are disclosed as foreign rows; the configured calendar is not retro-fitted onto them.
- It does **not** make the retention policy per-zone. Retention still prunes `validator_daily_snapshot` rows by age; the trend read reports what survived instead of pretending the window is complete.
- It does **not** claim a Provider timestamp the Provider does not send. The PlatScan detail envelope carries none, so in that path every point reads `sampleTime: "receipt"` with `delaySeconds: null` — and that is the honest reading, not a missing feature.
- It does **not** reconstruct the association intervals of a purged Node, and it does not re-attach them to a surviving Node; it reports `deletedNodes` and `associationHistoryPartial` and lets the retained days answer.

### Not asserted, with the reason

- No screenshot or pixel baseline: the ticket forbids `toHaveScreenshot` baselines and asks for measured geometry instead, which the second case asserts.
- `clamped: true` is unreachable from the panel's own presets (the largest asks for 365 days with `limit` 31, the clamp is 730 days); the spec asserts `clamped: false` and the clamp itself is covered by the Rust tests.
- The 180- and 365-day presets and a **second** `Load older days` step are not driven through the browser. The cursor mechanics they share with the 90-day path are asserted, and the page-size bound is unit tested.
- `clockSuspect` is not seeded — the PlatScan path carries no Provider timestamp that could run ahead of its receipt — so `validatorTrendDelay`'s suspicious-clock branch is unasserted. Its `Unknown` branch is asserted in both the unit tests and the browser.
- Asia/Kathmandu has no daylight-saving transition, so the 23/25-hour day the ticket names cannot be produced by this deployment; the browser asserts `24 hours` and the 23/25-hour path is covered by the unit tests over `validatorTrendDayWindow`.
- Server-only bounds (`limit <= 0` default, `limit > 366` clamp, an invalid `from`/`to` → 400, a pre-epoch `before` cursor) are covered by the Rust tests in crates/platpulse-server/tests/validator_trend.rs rather than through the panel.
- Behaviour worth stating plainly: a truncated page answers `limit + 1` configured local days, because `to` includes its own local day. The 90-day preset therefore legitimately reports `expectedDays 32 / observedDays 31 / missingDays 1` with `limit 31`; the stretch notice and `continuation` are what keep that honest on screen.
