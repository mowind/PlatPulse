### 15.33 Home toolbar: the row's two ends, one right-hand group, and the count that states itself (third-round Home refinement, delivered)

Applies to `PAGE-HOME-NETWORKS` (`/`), on top of §15.23, §15.24, §15.27, §15.30 and §15.32. It changes no route, no request, no DTO, no Server behaviour, no map geometry, no summary tile, no card size and no Emerald colour: it is the toolbar's own arrangement, one result-line reading (removed again by §15.35), one footer tone and the removal of one duplicate clear affordance. Where it revises a sentence above, it says so at that bullet; every other commitment of those sections stands.

- **The row has two ends, and the right-hand end is one group of three.** The toolbar (`data-slot="home-toolbar"`, platpulse-web/src/components/HomeToolbar.tsx) is `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-end gap-2 md:flex-nowrap` with exactly two children: the Network group (`data-slot="network-group"`, `min-w-0 grow overflow-x-auto`, the pills owning their own scroller and the free room in the row) and one actions group (`data-slot="home-toolbar-actions"`, `grid grid-flow-col grid-cols-[minmax(2.75rem,17rem)_auto_auto] items-center justify-end gap-2`) holding, left to right, the search control, the `Sort & filter` entry and the card/list View control. The three controls are one group pushed to the row's right edge by the Network group's own `grow`, not three separately right-floated items: there is no `ml-auto` per control and no room between them for the row to distribute. The group's own floor is its first track's least width: that track is `minmax(2.75rem, 17rem)`, so the least the group can contribute is the 44px of the icon entry the search control falls back to, and the group's automatic minimum size is exactly the min-content width of the three controls it holds, so the group can never be handed less room than its own children need — where the row is pressed, that floor is what keeps a control from being drawn outside the group, to the left of it and over the pills, which is what the row's right alignment would otherwise leave of it. That group is the unit that wraps below the tablet width and the unit the acceptance reads (`searchRoom` in platpulse-web/e2e/home-toolbar.spec.ts measures the room the row leaves the search control from that group's own children, subtracting the pills' group width and the row's gap only when the group has not wrapped onto its own line).
- **The wrap follows the row's own width, and the search control is the only thing that gives.** Below the tablet width the row wraps at its children's flex base sizes (the pills' content width plus the actions group's own 17rem field and the two controls beside it), so a phone row is the pills on one line and the three controls on the next; from the tablet width up the row never wraps (`md:flex-nowrap`), so 768x1024, 1280x800 and 1440x900 each keep one line and the room the row has left is settled by shrinking rather than by wrapping — a row that could hold both ends on one line is never drawn as two. Where the row has less room than its children ask for, the two groups give first: the Network group scrolls its own pills rather than truncating them (`min-w-0 grow overflow-x-auto`), inside the actions group the only child that gives is the search slot — the group's own first track, `minmax(2.75rem, 17rem)`, whose least width is the 44px of the slot's own icon entry — while both entries and the View control are `shrink-0` (the `Button` base class in platpulse-web/src/components/ui/button.tsx) and the group itself stops at the width its three controls' own min-content needs, which is why the search control is the first thing to change shape rather than any control losing its own size: and the shape it holds is still decided by §15.30's one `@container (min-width: 16rem)` rule and nothing else. That 17rem is the first track's maximum rather than a width laid on the slot, and the difference is the group's floor: a width is also the smallest contribution a box can make, so a 17rem-wide slot would put the group's own minimum at 17rem plus the two controls beside it and leave the Network group to pay every shortfall — a reader would keep a field in a row with no room for one, which is the reading §15.30 forbids. A track states the ask and the floor in one declaration: at its 17rem maximum the group asks for the field and the two controls beside it, so a row with the room draws the field, while at its 2.75rem minimum the group stops at the three controls' own minimum and the shortfall is handed to the Network group instead. The slot keeps `min-w-11`, which is that same floor restated on the child, and takes the track's width rather than asking for one of its own — a slot that asked for 17rem itself would make that ask its own minimum and put the group's floor back at 17rem. The slot asks for 17rem rather than §15.30's bare 16rem so that the row's room and the container query cannot meet on the same pixel: 16rem is the least a field needs (13rem of text, the 44px clear action, the 4px between them) and the slot asks for one rem more, so a row that has exactly the least room still shows a whole field instead of a field and a container-query decision disagreeing at the boundary. The field's own box is the whole 17rem — 272px, a readable search box inside the 220-280px the refinement asked for — because its `Clear search` action is drawn inside that box (`pr-11` while there is a word) rather than beside it, and `HOME_SEARCH_TRACK` (`16 * 16`, platpulse-web/e2e/helpers.ts) and `SEARCH_FIELD_TRACK` (the component's own resize comparison) both stay that 16rem floor, because they answer "has the row the least room for a field", not "how wide is the slot".
- **One clear action per search control, drawn only while there is a word to clear.** The row's own field carries the `Search` line icon inside its own box (`pointer-events-none absolute left-2.5 size-4`) and the `Clear search` `Button` (`data-slot="home-search-clear"`) pinned inside the field's right edge, rendered only while `queryText` is not blank: with an empty field there is no clear affordance at all, so the reader never meets a disabled control and never meets two ways to clear one word. The clear affordance a `type="search"` input draws in its own shadow DOM is removed for both search fields (`[data-slot='home-search-field']::-webkit-search-cancel-button, [data-slot='home-search-panel-field']::-webkit-search-cancel-button { -webkit-appearance: none; appearance: none; }`, platpulse-web/src/styles/emerald.css), because it is a second clear entry inside the same box that the theme cannot name, size or paint. The field reserves the action's room only while the action is drawn (`pr-11` with a word, `pr-2` without), so the text never runs under the button and never leaves a hole where no button is. The placeholder is `Search nodes...`; the accessible name stays `Search Active Nodes`, and the surface's own field keeps that same name, that same placeholder and that same draft (§15.30's "the two shapes are one control" is unchanged).
- **The word the reader typed stays in force while they leave the field.** The box keeps its own draft until the address bar carries the same text (`queryText = queryDraft ?? filters.query`, and the blur drops the draft only once `filters.query` *is* that same text, platpulse-web/src/components/HomeToolbar.tsx; #265). A keystroke updates the draft immediately and commits the URL with 150ms debounce (§15.30), so dropping the draft the moment focus leaves would read an empty query back for one frame: the field would show the reader's own text gone, the two marks that state an active search would leave the row, and the `Clear search` the walk below puts between the field and `Sort & filter` would vanish exactly as the reader's Tab reaches for it — a reader who types and tabs on has their query flicker out from under them, and the walk they were told to expect ends on nothing. The draft never outlives the address bar's agreement: one effect drops it as soon as `filters.query` is empty or already carries the text the box typed — so a commit that lands after the reader has left the field can never leave a draft behind — `popstate` drops it the moment the address bar lands anywhere, and `Clear search` drops it with the query itself, so the URL stays the one owner of what is in force (§15.30).
- **The status-filter chip is drawn only while it has a count.** §15.27's chip (`data-slot="home-filter-count"`) now appears only while one or two status filters are on, and at zero the entry draws no chip at all. The number still reaches every reader as the entry's own `aria-describedby` sentence (`#home-status-filter-count`, `<n> of 2 status filters on`) and inside the surface, and `Sort & filter` is still the entry's accessible name: the chip stays `aria-hidden="true"` decoration, so the zero case loses a mark rather than a statement.
- **The result line says the one number it lists, or both numbers when they differ.** §15.23's result line reads `<n> nodes` while neither the search nor a status filter narrows the list, and `Showing <matching> of <in-scope> nodes[ in <Network>].` as soon as one of them does (`listNarrowed` in platpulse-web/src/components/HomeDashboard.tsx). Every other reading is unchanged: the same in-scope denominator, the same Network scope name, the same `role="status"`/`aria-live="polite"` announcement of the change, the same two empty states, and no change to any count, filter, sort or scope. A line reading `Showing 7 of 7` under a band that already states seven is one sentence twice, while `7 nodes` under a narrowed list would hide the denominator the reader needs, so the short reading is used exactly where there is nothing to compare it with. §15.35 removes that line entirely — Home states how many Nodes a selection holds in the overview counters alone and the list carries no count of its own in either state, and `listNarrowed`, which existed only for the wider reading, is deleted with it.
- **The card footer's one word for a confirmed non-Validator is painted in the footer's own quiet tone.** §15.32's single word stays the whole statement (`validatorIdentityStatement`, platpulse-web/src/components/LinkedValidator.tsx), and its label is now `text-muted-foreground` for a confirmed non-Validator while every unconfirmed case keeps the `text-foreground` voice of the identity line: the word is a footnote on a card whose verdict the header already carries, and it must not read as loudly as the identity statement of a Node whose identity is not established. Nothing else on the card changes — same size, density and responsive column count, same Node Health, Validator Activity and Observed Network Head semantics, and CPU is still never the reason a Node is called Unhealthy.
- **The walk follows the row, not the order the sections above delivered.** With both surfaces closed the walk is Network (one tab stop) → the search control (the row's own field, or the entry that opens that same field) → `Sort & filter` → the card/list View control → the first filtered card; where the row shows its own field, the field's own `Clear search` is the stop between the field and `Sort & filter` while there is a word to clear. §15.23, §15.27 and §15.30 each carry this as a revision at their own bullet.
- Acceptance. The arrangement is held by platpulse-web/src/components/HomeDashboard.test.tsx (`gives the row two ends: the Network group and one right-hand group of three controls`: the row's two `data-slot`s in order, and the actions group's `home-search-slot` → `sort-filter-entry` → `tabs`), the absent chip by the same file's toolbar cases (no chip element at zero with the described-by sentence still present; the result-line readings this sentence used to name are removed by §15.35), the footer tone by platpulse-web/src/components/LinkedValidator.test.tsx (the muted word for a confirmed non-Validator, the `text-foreground` word for an unconfirmed one), and the browser acceptance by platpulse-web/e2e/home-toolbar.spec.ts (the row's room read against the container query at all five fixed viewports, the walk in the order above, one clear action per control and none at all on an empty field, no chip at zero, both surfaces below their own entries, and Light and Dark with 44x44 targets and no horizontal overflow — including the walk itself: a word typed and tabbed away from without a pause is still in force and its `Clear search` is still the next stop, which is what holds the draft rule above; and the arrangement's own floor, read as `keeps its three controls inside their own group when a long Network list presses the row`: with the pill list lengthened in the page's own DOM to a length this deployment's own data would have, the pills are the group that scrolls, no control is drawn outside the group's own box on either side, every control keeps its 44px, the row's two ends never overlap, the group still ends at the row's right edge and the pressed row hands the field back to its entry instead of keeping a field in room that cannot hold one), platpulse-web/e2e/home-search-filters.spec.ts (the list's own card count read against the summary's `Active Nodes` figure, and the keyboard walk in the new order) and platpulse-web/e2e/home-sort-keys.spec.ts (the narrowed readings unchanged).
